# Sound credits

Every sound here is **public domain or CC0** (no attribution required), from
Wikimedia Commons. The files in `effects/` are short cuts (1–6 s, faded,
loudness-matched) of these sources; `esphome/sounds/chime_*.flac` are sub-second
cuts of the same sources, used as the wake-word chime.
