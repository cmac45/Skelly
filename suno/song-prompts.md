# Skelly's songs — Suno prompts

Use Suno's **Custom** mode: paste **Style** into the Styles box and **Lyrics** into
the Lyrics box. No artist names (Suno rejects them). Generate two takes of each and
keep the best. Download as MP3 and copy to `/media/skelly/songs/` on Home Assistant as
`<name>.mp3` (full) and `<name>_short.mp3` (a ~1 minute cut ending after the first
chorus) — the names `play_song` expects are in `homeassistant/custom_components/skelly_tools`.

Family names are deliberately left out: these play to strangers on the driveway.
The gravestone jokes ("Rest in Puck", the uneaten veggies) are already public.
